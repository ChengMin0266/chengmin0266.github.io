Selected Games

★ Selected · London Games Festival 2024

Interstellar Adventure is an RPG Sokoban game. The game combines traditional box-pushing with four unlockable abilities (Break Ice, Thorn Phase, Pull Box, Teleport) and an explicit positive/negative feedback-loop system that adapts the difficulty to player performance. I led the core gameplay loop, all 24 level layouts, the difficulty progression, and the narrative scaffolding that ties the systems to the story.

Interstellar Adventure

Role
Core Loop & Level Designer
Engine
Unity
Year
2023
Team Size
3 people

Key Features

  • Paired positive + negative feedback loops keep the late game from going stale.
  • Surrendering a skill repairs a ship panel — losing power feels like progress.
  • 24 levels: a 7-level tutorial, 16 skill levels, and a final escape stage.
  • UAL MA Game Design final major project.
  • Exhibited at Game Day X during the 2024 London Games Festival.

Cook Human On Time is a Unity simulation that stages the idea of the social clock inside a kitchen. You play a Great Chef — and Great Parent — preparing human ingredients against the clock: washing each one in the sink (school) and cooking it in the pot (work), so that every life is served on time.

Cook Human On Time

Role
Game, Narrative & UI/UX Designer
Engine
Unity
Year
2023
Team Size
3 people

Key Features

  • Stages the 'social clock' as a kitchen — life milestones become ingredients cooked on time.
  • Time management under pressure: juggle several lives against tightening deadlines.
  • Deliberately clean UI, so players feel the clock — not the clutter.
  • Original concept and central metaphor; led game, narrative & UI/UX design.

Tarduck is a top-down extraction shooter I designed and rapidly prototyped — a fully playable proof-of-concept for validating an entire extraction-shooter loop end to end. Instead of spending weeks on a traditional vertical slice, I drove an AI-assisted 'vibe-coding' workflow to get from design doc to a running, systemic build in days, then iterated on game feel directly in the browser.

  • Grid inventory & stash economy with real risk/reward.

Respond to Signal 404 is a top-down campus puzzle-horror game built in Unity. Kept behind for after-school cleaning duty, you put on a pair of glasses you 'accidentally' found — and the empty school starts to change. Hidden signals surface, classrooms that shouldn't exist appear, and something refuses to stop following you.

Invisible Egyptian is a cross-medium puzzle game in which a digital game and a physical printed journal must be used together. You play Malek, an Egyptian archaeologist exploring an ancient tomb, deciphering hieroglyphs to uncover its secrets — and many of the answers live not on the screen, but in a companion book you hold in your hands.
