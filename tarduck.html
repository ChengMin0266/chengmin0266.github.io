Tarduck

Quack. Loot. Extract. A top-down extraction shooter about looting hard and getting out alive.

Role · Game Designer · Web · Three.js · 2026 · Top-Down Shooter

How to Play

Move WASD
Aim Mouse— character faces the cursor
Fire LMB
Aim assist RMB— tightens spread
Sprint Shift— consumes stamina
Crouch Ctrl— slower, faster stamina regen
Switch weapon 1–5
Reload R— auto on empty mag
Heal H— uses best healing item
Loot / Extract E— open corpse/crate · hold to extract
Open / close door F
Inventory Tab
Pause Esc

About

Tarduck is a top-down extraction shooter I designed and rapidly prototyped — a fully playable proof-of-concept for validating an entire extraction-shooter loop end to end. Instead of spending weeks on a traditional vertical slice, I drove an AI-assisted 'vibe-coding' workflow to get from design doc to a running, systemic build in days, then iterated on game feel directly in the browser.

Beneath the surface sits a deliberately deep systems layer: locational damage with limb fractures (a broken leg kills your sprint, a broken arm widens your spread), encumbrance that pits loot greed against mobility, a talent tree with build-defining combos, a grid inventory and stash economy, and the signature risk/reward of 'one more crate, or extract now?'. The prototype existed to pressure-test exactly that tension until the core loop bit.

The point was never finished art — it was to prove the design holds up as a game. Owning the full loop end to end (combat, loot, damage model, extraction) through an AI pipeline let me validate the concept with real play in a fraction of the usual time, and fail fast where it needed to. Co-developed with Ziwei Su.

Roadmap

The current models were built the fastest way possible to reach a playable build — the priority was getting the systems in front of real play, not the art. With the core loop proven, the next phase is production: a full art pass swapping the prototype assets for bespoke models and a proper Tarduck identity (yes, an actual duck), animation and sound polish, an expanded weapon and map roster, and balance tuning driven by real playtest data.
